## Indirect reporting

- `Diagnostic.unresolved` supplies the “Unknown …” and “Missing module …”
  templates. `ConversionResolver` reports mismatches with the supplied code and
  a context label or argument position. Argument coercion and context-free
  coercion default to E1009.
- `NullSafety` selects E1005 or W1003 from the configured mode. The pattern
  typer also reuses the identifier from an unknown-variable diagnostic.
- `AmbiguousName.describe` is checked in the diagnostic constructor. Any
  matching marker yields E1029 at the original diagnostic location.
- Parser, conditional compilation, embedded text, and HXI helpers take messages
  from their callers. RPC validation passes its messages to E1024 through a
  callback. Shader errors and warnings and the editor's E0000 use separate
  output paths. Symbolic editor statuses and the C++ subset validator's CXX
  identifiers use their own protocols.

## Possible follow-ups

The strongest candidates for separate codes are E1002 (assignment, inference,
declarations, and scoped lifetimes), E1005 (lookup and null safety), E1007
(declaration and calling rules), E1019 (pattern typing and deferred control flow),
E1020 (name/declaration resolution and duplicate cases), E1021 (exhaustiveness,
throwing, and extern declarations), E1022 (native ABI and catch clauses), and
E1024 (metadata, RPC/wire, and field writes). E0001, E0002, E3001, E1003, E1008,
E1009, E1010, E1014, E1016, and E1025 also cover families of checks.

The published reference uses one page with an anchor for each code. This keeps
related short entries searchable together and gives each diagnostic a direct link.
